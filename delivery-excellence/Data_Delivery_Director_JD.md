# Job Description – Delivery Director (Data)

## Role Summary

The Delivery Director leads a large Data & AI portfolio, overseeing Architects, Tech Leads, Engineers, QA, and contractors to ensure high-quality delivery, customer success, financial governance, and productivity improvement. The role is customer-facing, drives cross-sell/upsell, manages rebadging and capacity programs, and ensures execution excellence while promoting automation and GenAI-enabled efficiencies.

**Location:** Bengaluru, India

## Key Responsibilities

### 1. Delivery Leadership & Governance

- Own end-to-end delivery across multiple data, platform, and AI workstreams.
- Lead day-to-day execution, delivery governance, risk management, and quality assurance.
- Ensure alignment with architecture, engineering best practices, cloud standards, and Lakehouse principles.

### 2. Customer Engagement & Stakeholder Management

- Act as the primary delivery leader for customer executives and program owners.
- Manage expectations, challenge constructively when needed, and communicate progress and risks clearly.
- Build long-term trusted relationships and drive customer confidence through predictable execution.

### 3. Rebadging, Capacity & Productivity Management

- Lead one-to-one rebadging, knowledge transition, onboarding, and stabilization of teams.
- Govern work classification for BAU (transfer work) vs transformation work.
- Implement capacity-based intake governance and scope control.
- Drive productivity improvements (e.g. targeting xx% in Year 1, scaling toward x% over multi-year engagement).

### 4. Financial Management & Commercial Ownership

- Own P&L, billing, resourcing, forecasting, and cost optimization.
- Ensure contractual compliance, SOW governance, and margin protection.
- Identify and close cross-sell and up-sell opportunities aligned to customer needs.

### 5. Technology, Automation & GenAI Enablement

- Provide high-level guidance across data engineering, platform engineering, Databricks, and cloud ecosystems.
- Promote automation-first delivery, including opportunities for agentic workflows and GenAI-driven productivity.
- Support tool modernization, platform adoption, and alignment with enterprise architecture.

### 6. People Leadership & Team Development

- Lead and mentor senior architects, tech leads, and large engineering teams.
- Foster a culture of accountability, learning, automation, and delivery excellence.
- Manage career development, coaching, and performance frameworks across the program.

## Required Qualifications

- 20+ years of IT experience with 10+ years in large-scale delivery leadership roles.
- Strong experience managing 100+ member teams across data, cloud, and AI programs.
- Experience in rebadging, team transitions, BAU vs transformation delivery models, and capacity governance.
- Strong financial management, customer handling, and executive communication skills.
- Working knowledge of Databricks, cloud platforms (AWS/Azure/GCP), and data engineering fundamentals.
- Exposure to GenAI, agentic automation, and platform modernization initiatives.
